Delete Your Young Hustlers Account

Young Hustlers, operated by 2812 Enterprises LLC, allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete your account inside the app

Signed-in users can request account deletion directly from the Young Hustlers app.

Go to Dashboard → Settings → Delete Account and follow the instructions shown in the app.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request deletion of your Young Hustlers account by contacting us from the email address associated with your account.

Request Account Deletion

Data that may be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, personal information and account data associated with the Young Hustlers account may be deleted, including profile information, account identifiers, user content, and other data associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or transaction-record purposes.

Retained information is limited to what is necessary for these purposes and is handled according to our Privacy Policy.
