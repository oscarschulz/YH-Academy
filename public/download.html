Before you join

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the app to get started?

No. Create an account in your browser, or download the app on iPhone or Android.

Is it free to download?

Yes. The app is free to download. Some features may require membership access or an in-app purchase.

Does creating an account give me access to every division?

No. Registering gives you an account. Some areas have separate access requirements. Check the division you’re interested in for its next steps.

Can I use an existing account?

Yes. If you already have a Young Hustlers Universe account, sign in rather than creating another one.

What if the app isn’t available in my store?

Availability may vary by device or region. You can get started on the web or contact support.
