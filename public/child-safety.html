Young Hustlers Child Safety Standards

Young Hustlers, operated by 2812 Enterprises LLC, is committed to maintaining a safe community and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Prohibited content and behavior

Young Hustlers prohibits content, conduct, or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, or facilitates harm to children or minors. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, exploitation, and any attempt to promote or facilitate such activity.

Reporting and safety controls

Young Hustlers provides reporting and safety controls in supported community and communication areas of the app. Users can report unsafe, abusive, or inappropriate activity and can block users where blocking controls are available.

Reports may be reviewed and appropriate action may be taken, including restriction or removal of content or accounts when violations are found.

Child sexual abuse material

CSAM is strictly prohibited. When we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse or exploitation, we may preserve relevant information, remove or restrict access where appropriate, and report matters to applicable authorities or organizations when required by law.

Compliance

Young Hustlers follows applicable child safety laws and takes reasonable measures to prevent, identify, and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation on the service.
